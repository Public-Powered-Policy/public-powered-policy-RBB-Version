Media

Podcast interviews and articles featuring the founders of Public Powered Policy.

For press inquiries, contact media@publicpoweredpolicy.org.

  • Big Voice Wednesday, hosted by Ronda Cress June 2026

    The Little Girl with the Big Voice (opens in a new tab)

    A podcast interview with Rebecca Bond and Kristin Stitcher for Ronda Cress's Big Voice Wednesday series, on how members of the public can use their voice to take part in government policymaking.

  • GovLove Podcast April 2026

    Making Digital Government ADA Accessible (opens in a new tab)

    An episode of the GovLove podcast in which Rebecca Bond and Kristin Stitcher discuss digital accessibility requirements under the Americans with Disabilities Act for state and local government websites and apps.

  • Justice Connection February 2026

    The Government Is Making Decisions for You Without You (opens in a new tab)

    An op-ed by Rebecca B. Bond for Justice Connection, on federal agencies making policy changes with limited opportunity for public comment, and the case for stronger public participation before those decisions are finalized.
