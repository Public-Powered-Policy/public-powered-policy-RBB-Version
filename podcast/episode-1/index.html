Episode 1 · October 27, 2026 · 24 min

Scam Ads Are Everywhere. What Should Platforms Have to Do About Them?

In our first episode, we look at an FTC request for public input on what responsibility online platforms should have when scammers use their advertising tools to impersonate banks, businesses, and government agencies. We explain what the FTC is considering, who could be affected, and how you can weigh in before comments close on November 30, 2026.

Episode 1 will be available here on October 27, 2026.

Rebecca Bond & Kristin Stitcher

Former U.S. Department of Justice civil rights attorneys who helped develop federal disability rights regulations.
