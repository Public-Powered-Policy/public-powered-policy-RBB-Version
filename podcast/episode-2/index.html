A New Tax Credit Will Help Pay for K‑12 Scholarships.
How Should It Work?

In our second episode, we look at how the federal government is writing the rules for a new tax credit that can help pay for K‑12 scholarships. We explain what the rules could cover, including how families prove their income, how tutors get paid, and what families learn before choosing a school, and how you can weigh in before comments close on December 1, 2026.
