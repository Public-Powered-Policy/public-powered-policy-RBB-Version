About the show

Regularly Speaking is a plain-language podcast about federal rulemaking: how agencies write the rules that affect daily life, and how the public can take part.

Each episode looks at a real rule or a piece of the process, using real-world examples to show how rules are written, how public comments can shape them, and how they affect people's lives. Every episode comes with a full transcript.

  • 1

    How rules get made

    The notice-and-comment process, explained step by step.

  • 2

    Rules that are open now

    What agencies are proposing and when comments are due.

  • 3

    Comments that made a difference

    Real examples of public input changing a final rule.

Your hosts

Rebecca Bond

Co-founder and Executive Director

Rebecca spent 25 years in the Civil Rights Division of the U.S. Department of Justice, including 12 as Chief of the Disability Rights Section. She led major rulemakings under the Americans with Disabilities Act.

Kristin Stitcher

Co-founder and Director of Policy and Programs

Kristin worked in the Disability Rights Section of the Justice Department's Civil Rights Division for more than eight years, including as Deputy Chief. She led the team that developed ADA regulations and technical assistance for the public.
